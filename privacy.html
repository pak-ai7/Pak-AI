Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Pak AI, founded by Mukesh Heerani (Prince Hyderabadi), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI Agent platform.

1. Information We Collect

Because Pak AI is an intelligent agent, it processes the information you provide to generate responses:

  • User Inputs: The prompts, questions, and text you submit to the AI Agent.
  • Account Data: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication data.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our agent, including device type, IP address, and browser metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Pak AI Agent.
  • To improve and personalize your experience.
  • To monitor and analyze usage trends and system performance.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support.

3. AI and Data Processing

Pak AI utilizes advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) via secure third-party APIs (such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Groq) to process your requests. We do not sell your personal data or private chat histories to third parties. Data may be processed temporarily to generate your specific response but is not stored by third-party providers for their own training purposes.

4. Data Security and Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security measures (including encryption in transit and at rest) to protect your data. We retain your data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may delete your chat history directly from the interface or request account deletion by contacting us.
